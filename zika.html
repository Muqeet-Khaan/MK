Zika Virus

Year Originated

First identified in 1947.

Location of Origin

Zika Forest, Uganda.

Spread of Virus/Disease

Primarily spread by Aedes mosquitoes; also via sexual transmission and from mother to fetus leading to birth defects.

Precautions Required

  • Mosquito control and protection, travel advisories for pregnant individuals.

Symptoms

  • Mild fever, rash, joint pain, conjunctivitis; many infections are asymptomatic.

Treatment

Supportive care (rest, fluids, acetaminophen); prevent mosquito bites to reduce spread.
