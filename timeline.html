Year Wise Listing

1918 – Spanish Flu (H1N1)

One of the deadliest pandemics, estimated 50 million deaths worldwide.

1957 – Asian Flu (H2N2)

Global pandemic with significant mortality; contributed to modern influenza surveillance.

1968 – Hong Kong Flu (H3N2)

Pandemic that spread rapidly, reinforcing the need for seasonal vaccination strategies.

2002 – SARS-CoV

Severe acute respiratory syndrome outbreak highlighting the impact of coronaviruses.

2009 – H1N1 Influenza

Swine-origin influenza pandemic; rapid global spread with relatively lower mortality rates.

2014 – West African Ebola

Severe outbreak with high case fatality rates, predominantly in Guinea, Liberia, and Sierra Leone.

2015 – Zika Virus

Associated with congenital microcephaly; spread across the Americas.

2020 – COVID-19

Pandemic caused by SARS-CoV-2, resulting in 7M+ reported deaths globally (WHO/OWID).
