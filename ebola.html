Ebola Virus Disease

Year Originated

First identified in 1976.

Location of Origin

Near the Ebola River in what is now the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Spread of Virus/Disease

Transmitted through direct contact with bodily fluids of infected people or animals; outbreaks have high case fatality rates.

Precautions Required

  • Infection prevention and control (IPC) measures; PPE for healthcare workers.
  • Safe burial practices and contact tracing.

Symptoms

  • Fever, severe weakness, vomiting, diarrhea; sometimes bleeding.

Treatment

Supportive care with fluids and electrolytes; monoclonal antibody therapies and vaccines have been used in outbreaks.
