Influenza (Flu)

Year Originated

Recognized for centuries; notable pandemics include 1918, 1957, 1968, and 2009.

Location of Origin

Various origins depending on strain and pandemic era.

Spread of Virus/Disease

Respiratory transmission via droplets and aerosols; seasonal epidemics and occasional pandemics.

Precautions Required

  • Annual vaccination, respiratory etiquette, hand hygiene.

Symptoms

  • Fever, cough, sore throat, aches, fatigue; complications can occur in high-risk groups.

Treatment

Supportive care; antivirals (e.g., oseltamivir) for specific cases; vaccination reduces severity.
