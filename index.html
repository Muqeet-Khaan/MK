Understanding Biological Viruses

Viruses are microscopic infectious agents that can cause diseases in humans, animals, and plants. They replicate inside host cells and come in various types.

Explore Types Learn More

Overview of Virus Types

DNA Viruses

Store genetic information as DNA. Examples include adenoviruses and herpesviruses. Often stable with lower mutation rates.

RNA Viruses

Use RNA as genetic material. Examples include coronaviruses and influenza; typically mutate more quickly.

Enveloped vs. Non-enveloped

Enveloped viruses have a lipid membrane aiding entry but sensitive to disinfectants; non-enveloped are generally more resilient.
