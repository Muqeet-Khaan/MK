COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2)

Year Originated

Identified in late 2019.

Location of Origin

Wuhan, Hubei province, China.

Spread of Virus/Disease

Primarily via respiratory droplets and aerosols; global spread led to the COVID-19 pandemic declared by WHO in 2020.

Precautions Required

  • Vaccination and boosters as recommended.
  • Masking in high-risk settings and good ventilation.
  • Hand hygiene and staying home when ill.

Symptoms

  • Fever, cough, fatigue, loss of taste/smell; may progress to difficulty breathing.

Treatment

Supportive care; antivirals (e.g., nirmatrelvir/ritonavir) for eligible patients; clinical guidance evolves with variants.
