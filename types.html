Types of Virus

DNA Viruses

Contain DNA as genetic material. Examples: Adenoviruses, Herpesviruses. Generally more genetically stable.

RNA Viruses

Contain RNA. Examples: Coronaviruses, Influenza viruses. Often show higher mutation rates.

Enveloped Viruses

Wrapped in a lipid membrane. Often sensitive to heat, detergents, and disinfectants.

Non-enveloped Viruses

Lack a lipid envelope and can be more resistant to environmental stressors and disinfectants.

Retroviruses

Use reverse transcriptase to integrate into host DNA. Example: HIV.

Adenoviruses

Commonly cause respiratory illnesses and conjunctivitis; studied for vaccine vectors.
